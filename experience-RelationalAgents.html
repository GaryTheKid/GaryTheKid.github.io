The lab

The Relational Agents Group logo: a human hand meets a robot hand

The Relational Agents Group builds and studies computer agents that simulate face-to-face counseling, mostly in health education and health behavior change, with a particular focus on the relational side of those conversations and how it unfolds over time. The lab has spent about two decades on relational agents, from bedside virtual nurses for patients with low health literacy to long-term exercise coaches.
What I do here
I joined in 08/2024, when the lab needed someone to own its virtual reality work, and I now lead the VR and LLM side of its patient education agents. My research question grew out of that work: why can an agent tell a patient everything in a pamphlet and still not teach it? The projects below are the parts of the Problem Map I have pushed on so far.
  • Dr. Clara: an LLM-driven embodied agent that turns patient education pamphlets into guided VR lessons (under review).
  • Presence Is Not Pedagogy: a pedagogical layer that takes teaching decisions out of the language model (ongoing).
  • Teach-back with an LLM judge: checking understanding instead of assuming it (under review).
  • Life-sized VR health coaches: rapport and physical distance in VR counseling (IVA 2026).
